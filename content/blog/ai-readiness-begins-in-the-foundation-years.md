*The Unseen Curriculum, Part 1.*

How do we prepare a four-year-old for an AI world? By strengthening what stays human: talking and listening, wondering, playing, negotiating, persisting, and asking **"Why? What if? How do I know?"**

The principle is simple: **AI in the teacher's hands. Childhood in the child's.**

The piece also looks at a 2025 PNAS study, in which secondary students using a general AI chatbot did 48% better in practice and 17% worse once the AI was removed, alongside PISA 2025 and ASER 2024.
