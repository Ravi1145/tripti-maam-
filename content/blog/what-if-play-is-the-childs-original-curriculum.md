Drawing on Peter Gray's work, this reflection starts from a simple observation: animals practise for life through play.

So we can ask of children, too: **what future capability are they rehearsing?**

Children today get less free, self-directed play than earlier generations. That makes the question more urgent, not less.

> If play is nature's curriculum for preparing children for life, what happens when we replace too much of it with our own?
