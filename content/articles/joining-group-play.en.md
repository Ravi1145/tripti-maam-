## A familiar moment

At home your child is the first to start a game. At school, a teacher tells you they stand at the edge and watch. You wonder if something is wrong.

Often nothing is. Many children need to see how a game works, who is in charge and what the rules are before they step in.

## One thing to try

At a park or a gathering, stay close and say, in a few words, what the other children are doing: "They are filling the bucket." Then offer one small way in: "They might need someone to hold the other handle."

Let your child say yes, or keep watching a little longer. Both are fine.

## What to notice

Does your child watch, copy a movement, carry a prop, or speak first? Watching closely is already a way of taking part.

If you can, share what helped with their teacher, so the same way in can be offered at school. This is also something I look for when I observe classrooms.
