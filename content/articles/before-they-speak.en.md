## A familiar moment

You are dressing your baby. You say, "Shall we put on your blue top?" and then, without meaning to, you pause. Your baby looks at your face. A small sound comes out. Their hands move.

That pause is where the conversation happens. Your baby has just taken a turn.

## One thing to try

Pick one short phrase you already say every day. Say it, then count a slow three in your head. Whatever your baby does, answer it. If they look, smile. If they make a sound, copy it or say it back with a word. If they go still, wait a little longer.

You do not need to say anything clever. Your face and your voice are enough.

## What to notice

What does your baby do first when you pause? Does it change when you use a different phrase, or when someone else does it? A baby who looks, then makes a sound, then waits for you is already doing something very like talking.
