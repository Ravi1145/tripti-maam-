## A familiar moment

Your baby is on your lap at a meal. Their hand goes out towards your spoon, then to the cup, then back to the spoon. They are not hungry for the spoon. They are curious about it.

## One thing to try

Offer one safe, everyday object that is large enough not to be a choking risk, such as a wooden spoon or a soft cloth. Hold it within reach, and then wait. Let your baby decide whether to touch it, grasp it or push it away.

Stay close, and keep watching. Safety always comes first, especially with young babies and anything small.

## What to notice

Which object does your baby reach for again and again? Do they look at it, mouth it, bang it or drop it and look down? Each of these is a different kind of exploring, and a baby will often choose the same kind of exploring for days.

When you say, "You wanted the spoon", you give them the words for what their hand already knew.
