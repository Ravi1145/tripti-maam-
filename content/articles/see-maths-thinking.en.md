## A familiar moment

You ask, "What is seven plus five?" and your child says "twelve" almost straight away. It is right. But you are not sure how they got there.

## One thing to try

Say, "Show me how you got that." Put out a few counters or buttons, or hand them a pencil. Let them move things around or draw while they explain.

You are not testing them. You are curious.

## What to notice

Do they count everything from one, count on from the bigger number, or use something they already know, such as "six plus six is twelve, so this is one less"? Two children can give the same correct answer and have worked in very different ways.

If you can, ask a second child, or someone else at home, to try a different way, and compare. Different ways are worth talking about.
