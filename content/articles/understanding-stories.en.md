## A familiar moment

You have just finished a short story at bedtime. Your child says, "That was good," and you wonder what they took from it.

## One thing to try

Ask, "What happened first?" Let your child answer in whatever way suits them: telling you, pointing at the pictures, or using a few toys. Then ask what one character might have been feeling, and why.

Use the language your family speaks most easily. Children usually make the best sense of a story in the language they think in.

## What to notice

Do they keep the order of events? Do they add how someone felt? Do they ask a question of their own, or link the story to something at home?

Any of these tells you they are doing more than listening. They are making the story their own.
