## A familiar moment

You are about to set the table. Your child comes over and reaches for the cups. You could do it in half the time. But they want a part in it.

## One thing to try

Give your child one small job that sits inside the routine: carry one unbreakable cup, choose where the spoon goes, or wipe one spot. Let them finish it their own way, even if the cup ends up in the wrong place.

Then say what they did: "You put every cup on the table."

## What to notice

Do they copy what you do, add a step of their own, or look to you to check? Each tells you something different. Copying shows what they are watching. Adding a step shows they have an idea. Checking shows they care how it is received.

Over a few days, notice what job they ask for again.
