## A familiar moment

Three children have set up a shop with a few boxes and some leaves for money. One is the shopkeeper. Two want to buy. Both reach the counter at once.

## One thing to try

Stay nearby and wait. If the children get stuck, ask a question rather than giving the answer: "How will the shopkeeper know whose turn it is?" Let them decide, even if their rule is different from the one you would have chosen.

## What to notice

Do they make a line, take turns by age, or let the shopkeeper choose? Do they need adult help to agree, or do they sort it out between themselves? Do they count, give change or use words like "next" and "my turn"?

A small shop is full of language, number and negotiation, and children are practising all three at once.
