## A familiar moment

Your toddler wants to close a box. One corner of the lid keeps lifting. They press it, turn the box, and then push it towards you.

Your hand may already be reaching out before you notice what they have tried.

## One thing to try

Next time, give them a little more time. Then, when they push the box towards you, ask: "Shall I hold the box while you turn the lid?" Offer help with one part only, and let them do the rest. If they need it, show one move, and then give them the next turn.

Two large, light containers with matching lids are enough to practise with.

## What to notice

When they try again, watch their hands. Do they turn the lid sooner, press a different corner, or hold the box in a new way? Do they ask for help more clearly?

You can say what you saw: "You held the box with one hand and turned the lid with the other." It gives them words for something they have just worked out.
