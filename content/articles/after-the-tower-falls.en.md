## A familiar moment

Your child has been building a tall tower for a while. It leans, wobbles and falls. For a moment, nobody moves.

What happens in the next few seconds tells you a great deal.

## One thing to try

Before they start, ask: "What will you make first?" If the tower falls, wait. Do not rush to rebuild it for them. Then ask: "What would you like to try next?" Give them the choice of whether to rebuild, change something, or do something else.

## What to notice

Do they rebuild exactly the same way, change one piece, or ask for a different block? Each of these is a plan changing, even if the child does not say so.

If you can, take a photo of the tower before it falls. Looking at it together later gives your child words for what they were planning.
